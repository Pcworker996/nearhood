# #17: Map: overlapping markers hide Happenings at the same position

**State at export:** open · **Labels:** needs-triage · **Opened:** 2026-10-24

_Exported from the private project tracker on 2026-10-30._

---

Part of #2. Found during the #4 acceptance audit.

## Problem

When two Happenings share the same map position, the marker drawn on top covers the other one. A reader cannot select the covered Happening from the map. It is still reachable from the Nearby highlights list.

Observed: `tests/browse.spec.ts` "selecting a map marker opens its preview even when highlights are collapsed" times out once the test database contains a second published Happening ("Report near Eastbank footbridge") at the seeded footbridge coordinates. Playwright reports that the other marker's icon intercepts the click.

## Scope

- Any map position with more than one Happening, including published Happenings and pending Report pins.
- Not caused by Report processing. Matching may legitimately create a separate Happening at the same place.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reader can open every Happening at a shared position from the map, on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] The map shows that more than one Happening is at that position.
- [ ] The known-failure reproduction in `tests/browse.spec.ts` (added for #4) passes and its `test.fail()` marker is removed.

## Open decision

Which interaction to use, for example a chooser listing the Happenings at that position, or spreading the markers apart when selected.
