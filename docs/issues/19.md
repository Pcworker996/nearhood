# #19: Nearhood V1: Page matching so incomplete coverage can finish

**State at export:** open · **Labels:** needs-triage · **Opened:** 2026-10-26

_Exported from the private project tracker on 2026-10-30._

---

## Problem

When coverage is incomplete, matching for a Report can never finish. This happens when more than 20 Happenings sit within 1,500 m (`poolTruncated`), or a candidate has more than 3 Reports (`omittedEvidence`). Since D2-B removed the time filter, nothing ages out of the pool, so this becomes more likely as data accumulates.

Today ([#4](4.md), R3-P3 in `docs/proposals/ticket-4-review-fixes.md`):
- The Report stays in `clarification` with `incomplete_coverage`.
- The contributor can choose a shown Happening or leave the Report unassigned.
- Create-new is refused, because unassessed candidates or evidence might describe the same occurrence (#4 criterion 43).
- The copy no longer suggests refreshing, since a refresh cannot change the outcome.

Who is affected: contributors in dense areas or near long-lived Happenings. Their genuinely new situations can never become a Happening.

## Proposed direction (R3 option P1, not yet designed)

Paged matching. After a pass that ends uncertain only because of incomplete coverage, later processing requests assess the next slice of candidates, or the next slice of omitted Reports for plausible candidates. This keeps #4's rule of one provider call per request. The work stops when:
- a plausible candidate is found, which goes to review as today, or
- coverage is complete, so the normal decision rules apply, or
- a pass cap is reached (3 passes is illustrative), and the Report stays unassigned with its explanation.

## Must be decided before implementation

- How a pass's checkpoint and fingerprint combine with earlier passes, and what invalidates them when nearby evidence changes between passes.
- The pass cap and its latency budget. Phase 4 hosted timing should inform both.
- Whether omitted Reports of a candidate assessed `separate` also need assessing, or only those of plausible candidates.
- The matching version bump and the recovery path for reviews saved under single-pass matching.

## Acceptance (draft)

- [ ] A Report near 25 unrelated Happenings, with fixture assessments of `separate` for all, completes in at most 2 passes and creates a new Happening.
- [ ] A plausible candidate found on pass 2 goes to review. Create-new then follows the existing unshown-plausible rules.
- [ ] Reaching the cap leaves the Report unassigned with its explanation, and no Happening is created.
- [ ] Each process POST makes at most one provider call.
